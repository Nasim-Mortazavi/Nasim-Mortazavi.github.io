About

I am a computational neuroscientist working at the intersection of human electrophysiology, spatial learning, and statistical modeling. My research uses intracranial (SEEG) recordings from the human hippocampus to ask how memories of place and association are formed and consolidated during learning.

My background spans physics and neuroscience: I hold a master's in computational physics and trained originally in theoretical physics before moving into clinical and computational neuroscience. I build the full analysis stack for my work — signal processing in MATLAB, mixed-effects modeling in R, and electrophysiology pipelines in Python.

Research

Sharp-wave ripples and associative spatial learning

Human CA1 · SEEG · hippocampal dynamics

How ripple activity in human CA1 reorganizes during learning: post-learning ripple-rate ramping, posterior predominance, and the link between ripple rate and behavioral performance.

Localizing electrodes within hippocampal subfields

HippUnfold · CT–MRI coregistration

A quantitative method for placing intracranial contacts within hippocampal subfields, resolving CA1 channel assignment across subjects.

Long-axis organization
